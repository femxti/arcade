Touch — built for phones

Ghost HunterGhouls & more
A cemetery shooter rebuilt from the original 2010 ITU Flash source — werewolves, vampires, a tractor-beam of ghouls, three bosses and a seven-scene campaign.
Nova SquadronKeep It Simple
The straightforward version: dive-bombing formations, a tractor beam that steals your fighter, and the dual ship you get back for rescuing it.
Nova Squadron 80s
A fixed shooter written to 1983 hardware rules: one colour per sprite, four sprites per scanline, three square-wave voices.
Delta RunFly to win
Fly the river, mind the fuel. Throttle down over the barges, shoot the bridge, and the next sector opens.
Papmand SplashOuch
Dig, collect, and drop boulders on things. Butterflies burst into diamonds if you time it right.
